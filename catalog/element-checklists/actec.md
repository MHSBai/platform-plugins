# Element checklist: ACTEC Engagement Letters guide (estate planning)

Publisher: American College of Trust and Estate Counsel (ACTEC).

Terms status: restricted or unstated (Catalog v2.1 Section 6); the current published terms were not read in this stage and are to be confirmed before any use beyond this checklist.

Status: not yet compared with the form. The elements are MHSB's expectation of what the form covers, not a verified inventory.

Use: element checklist only. This file lists what the form covers, in MHSB's words. It contains none of the form's wording and is not a substitute for the form.

Scope: Engagement terms for estate planning, including joint representation of spouses.

## Elements covered

### Joint representation

- [ ] Identity of each client and that both are clients.
- [ ] How information shared by one client is treated between them.
- [ ] What happens if the clients' interests diverge.
- [ ] Consent of each client, recorded with date.

### Scope

- [ ] Documents to be prepared and the planning steps included.
- [ ] Items excluded, such as funding of trusts or tax returns.
- [ ] Who is responsible for retitling assets and beneficiary designations.

### Fiduciary and later work

- [ ] Whether the firm represents a fiduciary later, and on what terms.
- [ ] Annual review as an offer, not an obligation.
- [ ] Custody of original documents.

### Fees

- [ ] Flat package or hourly basis.
- [ ] What triggers additional fees.
