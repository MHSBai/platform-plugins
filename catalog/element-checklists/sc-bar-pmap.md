# Element checklist: South Carolina Bar Practice Management Assistance Program forms

Publisher: South Carolina Bar, Practice Management Assistance Program (PMAP).

Terms status: unstated (Research Brief F4: no terms stated); written terms are to be requested before any republication (Design v2.1 Section 8).

Status: not yet compared with the form. The elements are MHSB's expectation of what the form covers, not a verified inventory.

Use: element checklist only. This file lists what the form covers, in MHSB's words. It contains none of the form's wording and is not a substitute for the form.

Scope: General, criminal, domestic and personal injury intake; non-engagement and disengagement; engagement terms.

## Elements covered

### General intake

- [ ] Prospective client identity, contact and referral source.
- [ ] Matter type and a short statement of the problem.
- [ ] Adverse parties and their counsel for the conflict check.
- [ ] Key dates known to the client.
- [ ] Documents the client holds.
- [ ] Fee discussion and its outcome.

### Criminal intake

- [ ] Charges, case number and court.
- [ ] Arrest and offense dates and the arresting agency.
- [ ] Release conditions and the next court date.
- [ ] Co-defendants and their counsel.
- [ ] Prior record as stated by the client.

### Domestic intake

- [ ] Parties, children and their dates of birth.
- [ ] Prior or pending cases and orders.
- [ ] A safety question asked by a person, with the client alone.
- [ ] Income, assets and debts in summary.
- [ ] Goals.

### Personal injury intake

- [ ] Incident facts, injuries and treatment.
- [ ] Insurance of each party.
- [ ] Witnesses.
- [ ] Prior claims.

### Non-engagement and disengagement

- [ ] The scope that is declined or ended.
- [ ] The effective date.
- [ ] A prompt that time limits may apply.
- [ ] Return of documents and the file.
- [ ] Contact for questions.

### Engagement terms

- [ ] Identity of the client and the scope of representation.
- [ ] Fee basis and billing.
- [ ] Responsibilities of each side.
- [ ] Termination.
- [ ] File retention.
