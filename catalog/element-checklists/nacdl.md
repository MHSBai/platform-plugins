# Element checklist: NACDL criminal defense intake materials

Publisher: National Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers (NACDL).

Terms status: restricted or unstated (Catalog v2.1 Section 6); the current published terms were not read in this stage and are to be confirmed before any use beyond this checklist.

Status: not yet compared with the form. The elements are MHSB's expectation of what the form covers, not a verified inventory.

Use: element checklist only. This file lists what the form covers, in MHSB's words. It contains none of the form's wording and is not a substitute for the form.

Scope: Criminal defense intake.

## Elements covered

### Charge and case

- [ ] Name as charged and other names used.
- [ ] Charges, case number, court and judge.
- [ ] Offense and arrest dates and the agency.

### Custody and release

- [ ] Custody status, bail and release conditions.
- [ ] Warrants.
- [ ] Next court date and its source.

### Related persons

- [ ] Co-defendants and their counsel.
- [ ] Complainants and witnesses for the conflict check.
- [ ] Persons the client authorizes the firm to contact, and for what.

### Consequences and needs

- [ ] Citizenship, flagged for an immigration consequences review by the attorney.
- [ ] Interpreter need and language.
- [ ] Probation or parole status.
- [ ] Pending and prior charges as stated.

### Privilege

- [ ] A reminder that jail calls are recorded.
- [ ] Third-party presence during the interview.
