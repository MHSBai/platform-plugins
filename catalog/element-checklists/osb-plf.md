# Element checklist: Oregon State Bar Professional Liability Fund practice forms

Publisher: Oregon State Bar Professional Liability Fund (PLF).

Terms status: restricted (Research Brief F4: provided for Oregon lawyers only); republication terms not granted; to be confirmed with the publisher.

Status: not yet compared with the form. The elements are MHSB's expectation of what the form covers, not a verified inventory.

Use: element checklist only. This file lists what the form covers, in MHSB's words. It contains none of the form's wording and is not a substitute for the form.

Scope: Personal injury and workers' compensation intake; nonengagement taxonomy; firm AI policy topics.

## Elements covered

### Personal injury intake

- [ ] Identity and contact of the prospective client, with a safe contact method.
- [ ] Date, place and manner of the incident.
- [ ] Injuries, treatment to date and treating providers.
- [ ] Insurance of each party, with policy limits where known.
- [ ] Witnesses and evidence held.
- [ ] Employment and wage loss.
- [ ] Prior claims, suits or injuries.
- [ ] Prior counsel on the same matter.
- [ ] Conflict check names: adverse parties, insurers and related persons.
- [ ] A limitation-period prompt for the attorney, without a computed date.

### Workers' compensation intake

- [ ] Injury date or onset of an occupational condition.
- [ ] Employer, supervisor and the date notice was given.
- [ ] Insurer and claim number.
- [ ] Treating physician and employer-directed examinations.
- [ ] Wages and hours before the injury.
- [ ] Prior injuries, claims and attorneys.
- [ ] Denial letters and their dates.

### Nonengagement

- [ ] A statement that the firm is not representing the person.
- [ ] No opinion on the merits.
- [ ] A prompt that time limits may apply, without stating one.
- [ ] Return of any documents received.
- [ ] A referral source, where the firm offers one.

### AI policy topics

- [ ] Confidentiality of client information in AI tools.
- [ ] Verification of AI output.
- [ ] Supervision of staff use.
- [ ] Billing for AI-assisted work.
- [ ] Disclosure to clients where required.
