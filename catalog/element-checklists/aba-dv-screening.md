Publisher: American Bar Association, Commission on Domestic and Sexual Violence.

Terms status: restricted or unstated (Catalog v2.1 Section 6); the current published terms were not read in this stage and are to be confirmed before any use beyond this checklist.

Status: not yet compared with the form. The elements are MHSB's expectation of what the form covers, not a verified inventory.

Use: element checklist only. This file lists what the form covers, in MHSB's words. It contains none of the form's wording and is not a substitute for the form.

Scope: Safety screening in family law intake.

## Elements covered

### Conduct of the screen

- [ ] Asked by a person, never by an automated form.
- [ ] Asked with the client alone.
- [ ] The firm's own protocol governs the questions and the response.

### What the record holds

- [ ] That the protocol was followed, by whom and on what date.
- [ ] The resulting contact rules: permitted channels, times and persons.
- [ ] An absent rule is recorded as unknown, never as safe.

### Effect on outreach

- [ ] No outreach draft without a verified contact rule with verifier and date.
- [ ] Urgent routing to the responsible attorney.
- [ ] Protective orders noted as orders, with their dates as entered.
