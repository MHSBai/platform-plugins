# Co-design record: Workers' compensation

Status: hidden draft (Catalog v2.1 Section 3.11). Adoption: NOT ADOPTED.

This pack is hidden until an attorney in the practice co-designs its fragment. The draft in `fragment/fragment.yaml` follows the Catalog's field list and carries no legal conclusions: deadline categories are flagged statutory with a citation placeholder, or firm-set; flags are attorney-review flags; sensitive fields are tagged and excluded from scoring. No MHSB engagement in this practice supports the draft. The questions below must be answered by that attorney before any pilot.

## Questions specific to workers' compensation

1. Which notice, claim, reopening and appeal windows apply in the firm's state, and what citation does each carry?
2. Which board form governs the fee agreement, and how is the cap recorded?
3. What does the client responsibilities sheet contain, and who adopts its wording?
4. How is attendance at an independent medical examination confirmed, and through which channels?
5. What is the firm's rule on recorded statements to the insurer?

## Questions for every hidden pack

1. Which intake questions are asked before engagement, and which wait until after it?
2. Which answers are sensitive in this practice beyond the tags in the fragment, and should any be masked in reports as well as excluded from scoring?
3. For each statutory deadline category, which provision governs in the firm's state, and who records the citation and adoption at the sitting?
4. Which attorney-review flags are missing from the closed list for this practice, and what intake fact raises each one?
5. Does the canonical stage map match how the firm's platform names and orders its stages?
6. Which communication templates may be generated at all, and which must wait for attorney-authored wording?
7. Who is the attorney adopter, and what evidence (a firm's docket sample, an engagement) supports moving the pack to pilot?

## Exit condition

The pack leaves the hidden tier only when an attorney in the practice has answered every question above in writing, the fragment has been revised to the answers, the adoption record names that attorney with a date and version, and the change is recorded in CHANGELOG.md. MHSB does not answer these questions on the attorney's behalf.
